What's new in 1.5.0

  • One place for pull requests. Reviews is now the Team tab of Pull requests, next to Mine, Open and Closed, and the section works even with no repository open.
  • Mine across your repos. Mine lists your open pull requests in every repository you follow, and the repository you have open always comes first in Team and Mine.
  • Sections in your order. Drag the sidebar sections into any order, or use Move Up and Move Down from their menu; ⌘1 to ⌘6 follow the order you choose.

Improvements

  • The Pull requests count in the sidebar shows your team's open pull requests waiting for review, drafts left out.
  • Settings › Reviews is now Settings › Pull requests, with the repositories you follow and hide kept as they were.
  • The command palette also finds the open repository's pull requests, even when you don't follow it.
  • By default Pull requests now sits above Stashes, so ⌘4 still opens it; Stashes is ⌘5 and Conflicts ⌘6.
