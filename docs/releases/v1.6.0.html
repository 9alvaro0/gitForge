What's new in 1.6.0

  • Pull requests at a glance. Team and Mine group pull requests by project, each row says where it stands (ready to merge, CI failed, conflicts…), and you can approve, merge or open one in the browser right from the list.
  • Room for the diff. In History, Expand diff (⇧⌘↩) makes the diff take the whole view, and ⌥⌘↑ / ⌥⌘↓ step through the commit's files.
  • History your way. Column dividers follow the pointer, the header's menu shows or hides Graph, Author, Date and Commit, and the inspector opens and closes from the toolbar or with ⌃⌘I.

Improvements

  • The commit detail keeps its title and author in place while the files scroll, folds the message away under Details and moves the commit actions into a … menu, so the diff gets more room.
  • The inspector can grow as wide as the window allows, and dragging the inspector's or the diff's divider all the way closes that pane.
  • The Graph column keeps the width you give it; lanes that don't fit fade out at its edge, and a double-click on its divider fits it again.
  • A diff pane you close stays closed when you pick another commit.
